# INV-05 — Asymmetric Prior

### Statement
The Bayesian engine runs on interoceptive priors weighted by autonomic geometry. Autonomic geometry is asymmetric by structure and shaped by load history. Different prior distributions produce different manifold adjacency — different regions become structurally accessible. This is not talent. It is geometry.

### Forcing Move

If a population shows consistent discovery differences, map their autonomic geometry before attributing talent. The discovery pattern is a prior distribution readout.

### Precision Expansion

* **Precision 1:** Different people think differently or see things differently. *(Incomplete — describes the output, misses the mechanism entirely).*
* **Precision 2:** Different groups or individuals show distinct cognitive profiles and discovery patterns. *(Direction is correct, but the physical mechanism is missing).*
* **Precision 3:** Autonomic geometry shapes interoceptive priors $\rightarrow$ priors alter manifold adjacency $\rightarrow$ different manifold regions become structurally accessible. *(Mechanism named).*
* **Precision 4:** Structural asymmetries (such as left/right stellate ganglia wiring or carotid/perfusion architecture) produce fixed baseline interoceptive differences that weight the Bayesian prior distribution. *(Measurable physical variable identified).*
* **Precision 5 (Falsifiable Test):** Identical autonomic geometry produces systematically different manifold activation patterns and discovery paths under controlled conditions. If geometry does not determine prior distribution and reachability, the invariant breaks.
