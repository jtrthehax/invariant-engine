# Priority and Versioning

**Initial public release:** 2026-11-02

This repository is the canonical source for the invariant stack. The Tier-1 invariants (INV-01 through INV-06, PI-01) are stated as falsifiable boundary conditions and are not revised retroactively. Refinements appear as new versions with dated changelog entries. The derivation chain from Tier-1 to Tier-2 and Tier-2.5 is preserved in the repository history and in `DERIVATION_APPENDIX.md`.

If you are citing this framework, cite the version you used. If you are building on it, fork from the tag, not from `main`.

**Precedence claim:** The commit history in this repository, beginning 2026-11-02, is the priority record. A Zenodo DOI minted against the `v1.0.0` tag is the citation anchor. Subsequent releases mint their own DOIs and point back to the first.

**Contact:** Open an issue. Do not email for corrections — public record is the point.
